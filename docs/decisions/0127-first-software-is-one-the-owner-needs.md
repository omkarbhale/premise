# 0127. The first software through premise is one the owner needs, as early as possible

- Status: Accepted
- Partly supersedes: [0060](0060-first-software-does-not-hold-up-premise.md) (that the first software does not hold up building premise), and the "After this, the owner can run their own pick whenever they want" step of [0110](0110-two-sample-softwares.md)
- Scope: The plan
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner, agreeing to the assistant's recommendation: "All of what you said sounds fine". The assistant's recommendation was that the first software through premise is one the owner actually needs, as early as possible, before the later milestones are built, with the two sample softwares kept as extra proof.
- Source: [Conversation record, 2026-11-21](../record/conversation-2026-11-21.md).

## Context

- [0050](0050-first-software-is-the-owners-pick.md) made the first software the owner's pick. [0060](0060-first-software-does-not-hold-up-premise.md) said the owner decides when, and that building premise does not wait for it. [0110](0110-two-sample-softwares.md) had milestone 1 shown on two sample softwares, after which the owner can run their own pick whenever they want.
- The owner doubled back on the planning ([0126](0126-what-is-being-built-confirmed.md)), not wanting to rebuild later.

## Decision

- The first software through premise is one the owner actually needs.
- It goes through as early as possible, before the later milestones are built.
- The two sample softwares of [0110](0110-two-sample-softwares.md) stay, as extra proof.
- Which software it is remains the owner's pick, made when the owner wants ([0050](0050-first-software-is-the-owners-pick.md)).

## Reason

- No reason beyond the one in [0126](0126-what-is-being-built-confirmed.md) was recorded: "I don't want to rebuild it later." The assistant's recommendation was given in that conversation; the owner agreed.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Build the later milestones first and use premise on the owner's software afterwards ([0060](0060-first-software-does-not-hold-up-premise.md)) | Adjusted by the owner's agreement with the recommendation. |
| The two samples only | They stay, as extra proof, not as the only proof. |

## Consequences

- [0060](0060-first-software-does-not-hold-up-premise.md) and [0110](0110-two-sample-softwares.md) are marked partly superseded. What of them stands: building premise's milestone 1 still does not wait for the pick; the samples are still shown running in milestone 1.
- The roadmap now has the owner's first software going through premise before milestone 2 onward is built ([roadmap](../product/roadmap.md)). The pick is an [open question](../open-questions.md#open-with-the-owner): until the owner names it, nothing past milestone 1 follows from this record.
- Building is still on hold ([0061](0061-nothing-built-until-the-owner-says.md)).
