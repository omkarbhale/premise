# 0249. The owner's requests during the build are intake: sized and slotted, never at the cost of planning's structure

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: Making premise
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner: "Don't compromise quality or structure of planning because of intake to premise from me. (As premise will/should, haha)"
- Source: [The owner's later conversation of 2026-11-21](../record/later-conversation-2026-11-21.md), given after the owner agreed to the assistant's suggestions on scale and order ([0166](0166-any-software-of-any-scale.md)–[0179](0179-the-roadmap-is-reordered.md)), together with: "let me know when a small real software can go through this pipeline."

## Context

- The owner will keep making requests while premise is being built. They are the first intake premise gets.

## Decision

1. **The owner's requests during the build are intake.** Each is read, sized and given its place in the order, like any other piece of work ([0168](0168-any-piece-of-work-goes-through-the-same-line-at-its-own-size.md)).
2. **None is rushed in at the cost of the planning's structure or quality.** The plan's order, records and decisions are kept as they are, and a request that changes them is a decision record, not a shortcut.
3. **The owner is told when a small real software can go through the line.** That is the first real use ([0175](0175-premise-is-used-for-real-as-soon-as-milestone-1-runs.md)).
4. **The owner's parenthesis is premise's own rule too:** intake does not break the structure of the work it comes into.

## Reason

- The owner, as quoted above.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Work on each request as it arrives | The owner asked for the opposite. |

## Consequences

- Requests during the build are slotted, with their reasons recorded, and the owner is told when the first real software can be run.
