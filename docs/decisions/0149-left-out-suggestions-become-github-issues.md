# 0149. Suggestions left out of a task go in as GitHub issues

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: Making premise
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner: "Everything left out of suggestions from workers can go in as gh issues, say because they are not in plan but reasonable from worker, or because of effort level."
- Source: The owner's standing addition of 2026-11-21, given while the foundation was being built.

## Context

- premise is built as a series of tasks ([0130](0130-the-build-starts-and-covers-all-of-premise.md)). Whoever builds a task may think of reasonable things that are not in the plan, or that are too much for the task at hand.

## Decision

- Each such suggestion goes in as its own GitHub issue on premise's repository: a short title, what and why, and the document or decision it relates to.
- The task itself is not widened for them.
- Issue text is public, like the repository ([0039](0039-public-repository.md)): it says "the owner" and holds nothing private.

## Reason

- The owner, as quoted above: suggestions are kept, whether they were left out for not being in the plan or for the effort.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Widen the task to include them | Against keeping each task to its scope. |
| Drop them | They would be lost. |

- Described in [how we work](../how-we-work.md#building-without-the-owner-from-2026-11-21).
