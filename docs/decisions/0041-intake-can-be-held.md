# 0041. Intake can be held

- Status: Superseded by [0062](0062-intake-goes-in-normally.md)
- Scope: The product
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Decided by: The owner: "I think that when lot of intake comes, (and we haven't decided "exactly" how that will work), but in any case, I think a lot of intake can be put on hold and later analyzed time to time to infer what people want, should vision of software and other things change, etc."
- Source: Old docs/decisions.md entry 41, which quotes the owner from the assistant's notes of the 2026-11-21 conversations; those notes are not kept in this repository.

## Context

- Said after the first records landed in this repository.
- How automatic intake works was still parked ([0018](0018-existing-software-and-automatic-intake.md)).

## Decision

- Much of what comes in through intake need not be acted on item by item.
- It can be held and analysed together from time to time.

## Reason

- Owner: "I think that when lot of intake comes, (and we haven't decided "exactly" how that will work), but in any case, I think a lot of intake can be put on hold and later analyzed time to time to infer what people want, should vision of software and other things change, etc."

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| None recorded | No alternative was put to the owner or recorded. |

## Consequences

- **Assistant's opinions given with it:**
  - some intake still needs acting on at once;
  - held is not ignored, so the sender is told;
  - each reading ends in a short list for the operator, who decides any change of purpose;
  - what triggers a reading and what skips the hold belong to the parked question of how automatic intake works.
- **Changed later:** the owner corrected this: intake goes in normally, and only what needs holding is held ([0062](0062-intake-goes-in-normally.md)).
