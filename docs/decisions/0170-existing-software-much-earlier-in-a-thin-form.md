# 0170. Existing software comes much earlier, in a thin form

- Status: Accepted
- Partly supersedes: [0034](0034-first-proof-new-software.md) (that existing software follows once the line works, after milestone 4)
- Scope: The plan
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner, on the assistant's seven suggestions: "I agree on 1, 2, 3. Put 4, 5, on later. I agree on 6, 7, and I agree on what you said to push later." (suggestion 1)
- Source: [The owner on scale, direction and order](../record/scale-and-direction-2026-11-21.md)

## Context

- Existing software was planned after the line works: milestone 6, after intake ([0034](0034-first-proof-new-software.md))
- The assistant suggested taking it on much earlier, in a thin form: draft the record from the code, strike what is wrong, and take intake from there.

## Decision

- Existing software is taken on much earlier, in a thin form: premise drafts the record from the code, people strike what is wrong, and later changes come in as intake.
- The fuller form (conflicts that need people, best-guess links from old code, a public open-source project) stays later.
- Order: after intake in a thin form ([0178](0178-the-roadmap-is-reordered.md)).

## Reason

- Owner: "I agree on 1, 2, 3."
- No reason beyond the suggestion itself was recorded: "thin: draft the record from the code, strike, intake from there".

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Keep existing software after intake and several roles | The owner agreed to bring it earlier. |
| Take it on in full form now | The owner agreed to the thin form; the full form (milestone 6) waits for what use shows. |

## Consequences

- The roadmap puts a thin existing-software step third ([roadmap](../product/roadmap.md)); milestone 6 keeps its number and is finished later.
- Builders' note (not the owner's): 0034's worry that existing software could swallow the effort is met by keeping the first form thin.
