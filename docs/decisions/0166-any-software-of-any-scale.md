# 0166. premise is for any software, of any scale

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: The product
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner: "Premise should, as its vision, work for any software. Be it a small tool, enterprise app, or a monorepo. How close we can get to that is engineering"
- Source: [The owner on scale, direction and order](../record/scale-and-direction-2026-11-21.md)

## Context

- The owner asked whether premise considered software of a much larger scale than one agent on one ticket: a module, a whole enterprise application, a monorepo.
- The vision already said premise "can be used for any software" ([0031](0031-the-product-is-general.md), [0049](0049-any-kind-of-software.md), [0057](0057-vision-stays-any-software.md)); those records are about the kind of software, not its size.

## Decision

- The vision's principle 9 gains one line: premise is for any software of any scale, from a small tool to an enterprise application to a monorepo.
- How close premise gets to that is engineering, not a limit written into the vision.
- VISION.md becomes draft 5.

## Reason

- Owner: "How close we can get to that is engineering".

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| A limit on size written into the vision | The owner put the limit in engineering, not in the vision. |
| Leaving the vision as it was ("any software") | It did not say scale, which is what the owner asked about. |

## Consequences

- [VISION.md](../../VISION.md) principle 9 and the draft line change.
- The pieces that make larger software reachable are [0168](0168-any-piece-of-work-goes-through-the-same-line-at-its-own-size.md) and [0170](0170-what-may-be-split-is-decided-by-strong-prompting.md).
- The vision states the vision only, with no now-or-later ([0035](0035-the-vision-states-the-vision-only.md)); how far premise has got is in the [roadmap](../product/roadmap.md).
