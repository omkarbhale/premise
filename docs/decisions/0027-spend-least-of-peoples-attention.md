# 0027. People's attention is what the product spends least of

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: The product
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Decided by: The owner, agreeing to the assistant's proposal: "I agree."
- Source: [Conversation record](../record/conversation-2026-11-20.md); old docs/decisions.md entry 27.

## Context

- The second of four points the prior-art research raised: human attention is the bottleneck, not build speed.

## Decision

- The product decides and shows its assumptions, not asks, whenever being wrong is cheap.

## Reason

- Assistant: "The evidence says build speed isn't the limit; people reading and answering is." "A short visible list of 'I assumed this' is cheaper to correct than a question is to answer."
- Owner: "I agree."

| Alternative | Why rejected |

## Consequences

- Vision principle 6: "People's attention is what the product spends least of. When being wrong is cheap it decides and shows its assumptions; when it is not, it asks."
- The say-back becomes a list to strike out ([0028](0028-say-back-as-assumptions-to-strike.md)).
