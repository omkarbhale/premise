# 0015. Some things are never traded for speed (accepted in principle)

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: The product
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Decided by: The owner, accepting the assistant's proposal in principle: "Maybe, yes, but when we make the whole plan of the software factory, we'll think how exactly that could happen or works."
- Source: [Conversation record](../record/conversation-2026-11-20.md); [shaping notes](../record/shaping-notes-2026-11-20.md); old docs/decisions.md entry 15.

## Context

- The assistant's fourth opinion: "The return-on-effort rule needs a floor."

## Decision

- A floor of things never traded for speed is accepted in principle.
- How it works is decided when the whole plan is made.

## Reason

- Assistant: "Some mistakes can't be bought back: lost data, a security hole, money spent, something sent out in the operator's name. I'd let the factory move fast on everything reversible and slow down only for those."
- Owner: "Maybe, yes, but when we make the whole plan of the software factory, we'll think how exactly that could happen or works."
