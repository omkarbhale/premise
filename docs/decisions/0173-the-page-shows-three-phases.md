# 0173. The page shows three phases, with the eleven stages as detail

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: The plan
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner, on the assistant's seven suggestions: "I agree on 1, 2, 3. Put 4, 5, on later. I agree on 6, 7, and I agree on what you said to push later." (suggestion 6)
- Source: [The owner on scale, direction and order](../record/scale-and-direction-2026-11-21.md)

## Context

- The page shows the line as eleven stages ([0154](0154-a-stand-in-line-until-the-line-engine-exists.md)); a person who has not read the guide sees a long list ([0135](0135-the-experience-works-without-the-guide.md)).

## Decision

- The page shows three phases: **Understand**, **Build**, **Running**.
- The eleven stages are the detail under them.
- Understand holds Why to Say back; Build holds Plan, Build and Check; Running holds Run, Report and Intake (builders' mapping, open to correction: the owner gave the three names, not the split).

## Reason

- Owner: "I agree on 6, 7."
- Builders' note (not the owner's): three phases tell a newcomer where a software stands without naming eleven stages.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Show all eleven stages at the top | The owner agreed to three phases. |
| Hide the stages | They stay as the detail; people may send a software back to a stage. |

## Consequences

- The page spec and the page change in milestone 1 ([page spec](../specs/page.md), [roadmap](../product/roadmap.md)).
- The line itself is unchanged ([the line](../architecture/the-line.md)).
