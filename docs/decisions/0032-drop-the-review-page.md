# 0032. Drop the review page for a short list of assumptions

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: Making premise
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Decided by: The assistant, after the owner's question "I don't know though what is the outcome of this page. I'm happy to anyway, just saying." The owner then answered the list that replaced the page; no objection to dropping it is recorded.
- Source: [Conversation record](../record/conversation-2026-11-20.md); [scope page draft](../record/scope-page-draft-0.1.html); [shaping notes](../record/shaping-notes-2026-11-20.md); old docs/decisions.md entry 32.

## Context

- The assistant had drafted the whole line as a review page to mark in, later or out (draft 0.1). It was never marked.
- **Earlier:** the owner had asked to keep it open ("Let's keep the lavish open, and we can come back to that later").

## Decision

- The scope page (draft 0.1) was dropped.
- The assistant put ten assumptions in chat for the owner to strike out; the outcome was the first vision statement.

## Reason

- Owner: "I don't know though what is the outcome of this page."
- Assistant: "most of it repeats things you've already told me. Making you read them again breaks the rule we just agreed about spending least of your attention."

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Refresh the page and have the owner mark it in, later and out | Most of it repeated what the owner had said; reading it again spends the owner's attention ([0027](0027-spend-least-of-peoples-attention.md)). |

## Consequences

- The page was closed when the vision statement replaced it. It is kept unchanged as a raw record.
- The answers to the ten assumptions are [0033](0033-answers-to-the-ten-assumptions.md).
- The page's table of who has the last word and its four risks were never discussed; the builders took them into the plan ([0084](0084-who-has-the-last-word.md), [architecture overview](../architecture/overview.md)).
