# 0012. Reporting is part of the line, with a product default

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: The product
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Decided by: The owner, agreeing to the assistant's proposal of a product default: "I agree".
- Source: [Conversation record](../record/conversation-2026-11-20.md); [scope page draft](../record/scope-page-draft-0.1.html); old docs/decisions.md entry 12.

## Context

- The owner put reporting in the line in the push-back on "the conversation is the product".

## Decision

- Reporting to the operator is in the line.
- Default report: what was decided, what is running, and what it needs from the operator (the scope page and vision add "and from whom").
- More detail when asked.

## Reason

- Owner: "if a software is big, the operator should be kept upto date in the level of detail he wishes to be kept (or same always as opinion of our product maybe)".
- Assistant: "Reporting should have a product default, not a setting to configure first."
- Owner: "I agree".

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| A level of detail the operator sets | Considered; the product default was chosen. |

## Consequences

- Vision line, step 10: "Report. What was decided, what is running, what is needed and from whom. More detail when asked."
- The report view and its rhythm are in [intake and reporting](../architecture/intake-and-reporting.md) ([0097](0097-reports-from-recorded-facts.md), [0098](0098-report-when-a-change-reaches-running.md)).
