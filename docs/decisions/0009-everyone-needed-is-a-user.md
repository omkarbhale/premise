# 0009. Everyone needed is a user of the product

- Status: Accepted
- Scope: The product
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Decided by: The owner: "all the roles needed are part of the target users for our product and the product gives a way for those other people to come in in those steps. It could be one person or many. Including the primary operator, which might or might not be just one person."
- Source: [Conversation record](../record/conversation-2026-11-20.md), the owner's reply marked "(*)"; old docs/decisions.md entry 9.

## Context

- The assistant had written "People other than the operator are named where an LLM can't reliably do the job." The owner widened it.

## Decision

- Owner: "all the roles needed are part of the target users for our product and the product gives a way for those other people to come in in those steps. It could be one person or many. Including the primary operator, which might or might not be just one person."

## Reason

- No reason was recorded.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Other people are only named where an LLM can't reliably do the job (the assistant's wording) | The owner widened it to every role being a user of the product. No further reason was recorded. |

## Consequences

- Vision, who it is for: every other person the software needs is also a user of the product, with a way in at their step, asked in their own terms.
- Where people come in was later settled as one open session, with roles as labels on what is asked ([0065](0065-one-open-session.md)).
