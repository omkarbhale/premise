# 0178. Hosting for other people, accounts and public deployment are pushed until two real softwares have run

- Status: Accepted
- Partly supersedes: [0033](0033-answers-to-the-ten-assumptions.md) (item 7: that other people setting premise up comes "sooner than fully deferred")
- Scope: The plan
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner, on the assistant's seven suggestions: "I agree on 1, 2, 3. Put 4, 5, on later. I agree on 6, 7, and I agree on what you said to push later." (the later items the assistant named)
- Source: [The owner on scale, direction and order](../record/scale-and-direction-2026-11-21.md)

## Context

- Other people setting premise up was to come sooner than the other later items ([0033](0033-answers-to-the-ten-assumptions.md), item 7, [0043](0043-runs-on-the-owners-machine-first.md)), with hosting and accounts decided then ([0045](0045-a-link-per-person-no-accounts.md)); keeping software live on public hosts followed.
- The assistant suggested pushing hosting for other people, accounts and public deployment until two real softwares have run through premise.

## Decision

- Hosting for other people, accounts and public deployment come last, after two real softwares have run through premise.
- That is milestone 5 (other people set premise up, hosting, accounts) and milestone 7's public hosting.
- Until then premise runs on the owner's machine, with no accounts. [0043](0043-runs-on-the-owners-machine-first.md) and [0045](0045-a-link-per-person-no-accounts.md) stand; what moves is when their later parts (hosting, accounts) are built.

## Reason

- Owner: "I agree on what you said to push later."
- No reason beyond the suggestion itself was recorded.
