# 0178. The roadmap is reordered

- Status: Accepted
- Partly supersedes: [0034](0034-first-proof-new-software.md) (that existing software follows once the line works, after milestone 4)
- Scope: The plan
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: The owner, in [0165](0165-any-software-of-any-scale.md)–[0177](0177-hosting-for-others-accounts-and-public-deployment-pushed-later.md): the order follows from them. How the order is written down (numbers kept, thin forms inside existing milestones, two new milestones) is the builders' choice, made here with the reasons below.
- Source: [The owner on scale, direction and order](../record/scale-and-direction-2026-11-21.md); the [roadmap](../product/roadmap.md) as it stood before.

## Context

- The roadmap was: milestone 1, then 2 (cost), 3 (every role), 4 (intake), 5 (other people set premise up), 6 (existing software), 7 (kept live, several softwares).
- The owner's decisions of 2026-11-21 move intake and existing software earlier, add measures and learning from corrections, add real use, and push other people's setup, hosting, accounts and public deployment to the end.

## Decision

The order of building, written plainly at the top of the roadmap:
1. **Milestone 1:** the thinnest line, now including work sized per piece ([0167](0167-any-piece-of-work-goes-through-the-same-line-at-its-own-size.md), [0169](0169-what-may-be-split-is-decided-by-strong-prompting.md)) and the three-phase page ([0173](0173-the-page-shows-three-phases.md)).
2. **Intake, in a thin form** (the first part of milestone 4).
3. **Existing software, in a thin form** ([0170](0170-existing-software-much-earlier-in-a-thin-form.md); the first part of milestone 6).
4. **The measures, and learning from corrections** ([0171](0171-premise-measures-its-own-return.md), [0172](0172-premise-learns-from-corrections.md); new milestone 8).
5. **Real use** ([0174](0174-premise-is-used-for-real-as-soon-as-milestone-1-runs.md); new milestone 9).
6. **Roles and taste (milestone 3), and cost, mocked (milestone 2).** The rest of milestones 4 and 6 are finished with them.
7. **Last, after two real softwares have run through premise** ([0177](0177-hosting-for-others-accounts-and-public-deployment-pushed-later.md)): other people setting premise up, with hosting and accounts (milestone 5), and keeping software live on public hosts (milestone 7).

Builders' choices (not the owner's), open to the owner's correction:
- Milestone numbers stay as they were, because many records and documents link to them. The two new steps are milestones 8 and 9; the order is stated at the top of the roadmap instead of by renumbering.
- Intake and existing software are each built in two parts inside their own milestone: the thin form early, the rest where the roadmap puts roles and cost.
- Use starts as soon as milestone 1 runs, whatever is next in the list ([0174](0174-premise-is-used-for-real-as-soon-as-milestone-1-runs.md)); milestone 9 is where use becomes the main work and the count of real softwares starts.

## Reason

- Owner: the decisions in [0170](0170-existing-software-much-earlier-in-a-thin-form.md) to [0177](0177-hosting-for-others-accounts-and-public-deployment-pushed-later.md).
- Builders: keeping numbers avoids breaking links across about forty documents; splitting two milestones into a thin and a fuller part gives the order the owner agreed without moving their scope into a new name.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Renumber the milestones to match the new order | It breaks many links and every earlier record's "milestone N". |
| New milestones for the thin forms of intake and existing software | The scope is the same work, split in time; one milestone per subject is easier to follow. |
